What we do

The Digital Global Health and AI group, led by Dr. Richard Lester, conducts rigorous research and data science to design, evaluate, and scale digital health interventions for the world’s most pressing health challenges. Our work bridges patient-engagement technologies, real-world clinical data, and emerging AI methods, with a particular focus on global infectious disease, maternal and child health, and underserved populations in British Columbia, Rwanda, Kenya, Uganda, and beyond.

Building on more than a decade of mobile health (mHealth) research, including the WelTel platform originally validated in HIV care in Kenya, we now apply natural language processing (NLP), machine translation, and large language models to two-way patient–provider communication data. These tools support real-time clinical decision-making, surface emerging health issues at the population level, and extend access to care in low-resource and multilingual settings.

Through methodologically rigorous trials, implementation science, and responsible AI development, our goal is to translate digital innovation into measurable impact on patient outcomes, equity, and health-system performance.

Featured projects

Patient engagement

WelTel

SMS-based patient engagement platform supporting HIV care, mental health, COVID-19 follow-up, and chronic disease management across multiple countries.

Canada · Kenya · Rwanda · Uganda
AI & NLP

Smart Text Analytic Tools (STAT)

AI-supported triage of two-way patient–provider messages using natural language processing and large language models, including machine translation for multilingual care.

Lead platform · Active
CHW digital health

Rwanda CHW Digital Health Programme

Cluster-randomised trial evaluating digital learning and decision support for community health workers, in collaboration with the Rwanda Biomedical Centre.

Rwanda · RBC partnership
BC clinical

Hospital-to-Home (H2home)

mHealth-supported transitions of care for high-risk patients in British Columbia, integrating SMS check-ins with discharge pathways.

BC · VCH · Providence
Maternal & child health

MNCH Digital Programmes

Multilingual mobile interventions to improve coverage and retention along the maternal, neonatal and child health cascade.
