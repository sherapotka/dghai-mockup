About

The Global Health & AI Lab, led by Dr. Richard Lester, conducts rigorous research and data science to design, evaluate, and scale digital health interventions for the world’s most pressing health challenges. Our work bridges patient-engagement technologies, real-world clinical data, and emerging AI methods, with a focus on global infectious disease, maternal and child health, and underserved populations in British Columbia, Rwanda, Kenya, Uganda, and beyond.

Building on two decades of mobile health research, including the WelTel platform first validated in HIV care in Kenya (The Lancet, 2010), we now apply natural language processing, machine translation, and large language models to two-way patient–provider communication. These tools support clinical decision-making, surface emerging health issues at the population level, and extend access to care in low-resource and multilingual settings.

Through randomized trials, implementation science, and responsible AI development, our goal is to translate digital innovation into measurable impact on patient outcomes, equity, and health-system performance.
